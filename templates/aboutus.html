About the Company

Chakora Hub Pvt. Ltd. is a multi-domain innovation, education, and technology company with a mission to uplift society through knowledge, creativity, and meaningful problem-solving.

We specialize in:

  • Automation & Software Development
  • Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning Solutions
  • Cloud Technologies & Infrastructure Consulting
  • Master Data Management (MDM) Services
  • End-to-End IT Project Implementation & Support

Alongside services, we also provide high-quality training that bridges the gap between learning and real-world application.

Our training includes:

  • IT Technologies (Python, Cloud, DevOps, Data Engineering, etc.)
  • Automation Frameworks
  • AI / ML Project Implementation
  • Photoshop, UI/UX Fundamentals, and Digital Design

We are also expanding into Creative & Cultural Education, enabling learners to grow in both technology and art.
